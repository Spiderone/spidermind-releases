# SpiderMind™ privacy policy

SpiderMind™ is a desktop app for Windows. It runs on your PC and keeps your data there.

## Google Calendar (SpiderDays™)

When you connect a Google account, SpiderMind™ asks Google for:

- your calendars, **read only** (`calendar.readonly`): to show your events in the app;
- your email address, name and profile picture (`openid`, `email`, `profile`): to tell your accounts apart.

What happens with it:

- The events are read directly from Google by the app on your PC, and kept on your PC only.
- The access keys Google gives are encrypted by Windows and stored on your PC only.
- Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. SpiderMind™ has no server, no ads and no analytics.
- SpiderMind™ never writes, changes or deletes anything in your Google account.
- SpiderMind™'s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
  [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
  including the Limited Use requirements.

## Usage statistics

SpiderMind™ counts how its features are used, to improve itself. These counts stay on your PC and are never sent.

## Updates

SpiderMind™ checks this repository for new versions. Only the download itself is requested; no personal data is sent.

## Removing your data

- Disconnect an account in SpiderMind™ (Settings, or the calendar page): its keys and events are deleted from your PC,
  and Google is told to end the access.
- You can also remove the access at any time from your Google account: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
- Uninstalling SpiderMind™ removes the app; its data folder is in `%APPDATA%\SpiderMind`.
